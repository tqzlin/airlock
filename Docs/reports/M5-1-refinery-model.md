## What the model does

The already verified run takes a booked cargo contract from the Rig onto the hauler, through cargo, air,
ship-system and fuel hazards, then back through the refinery docking card with pay and base fuel credit.
That baseline had 104 tests and a verified listen-server/client run. The new floor design makes Halder a
working destination: intact delivered casks become the input to three stills during a ten-minute slot.

This change supplies the pure model for that floor. A still takes a queued cask, meters crude through a
temperature-dependent diesel/gas/tar split, and credits the tank continuously. Feed cools the bath; heat,
vent and remaining charge determine pressure; calibration drifts only while feeding. Floods spill unfed
crude. A pop spills part of the remaining charge and discards the rest. A flash loses the charge and damages
the still. Coke can halve diesel yield without stopping feed, then fail the still and cut its heat. Reset,
purge, scrape, repair and calibrate have distinct preconditions and preserve unrelated fields.

The owner receives ordered events and the ppm generated by each tick. No actors, widgets, replication,
randomness, timers, console helpers, floor clock or section wiring were added. `Tally` reports product pay,
unsold crude, losses, ship refuelling and surplus base fuel without changing the model. The old playable
run behaves as before because nothing calls this new model yet.

Files:

- [RefineryModel.cpp](../../Source/Airlock/Ship/RefineryModel.cpp): deterministic operations, ordered slices, tally and strings.

## Constants and interpretations

All default numbers from the brief are retained. Physical quantities, rates and prices use `float`;
counts, knob steps, final credits and whole fuel tonnes use `int32`. Only `HopperCasks` and `KnobSteps` are
integer rate fields. These are the complete default groups and the interpretations used:

| Group | Defaults | Interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Batch and timing | `CaskLitres=1000`, `MaxFeedLps=20`, `HopperCasks=3`, `KnobSteps=20`, `MaxStepSeconds=0.25`, `SlotSeconds=600` | Three queued casks plus one loaded; integer steps are 5%. A positive call is sliced at most every 0.25 s, slices outside and stills in build order inside. Slot length is exposed for the owner, not an internal countdown. |
| Bath | `HeatBaseK=92`, `HeatSpanK=260`, `HeatTauSeconds=10`, `FeedCoolKPerL=0.12` | The explicit first-order update from the brief, then a 92 K floor. Cooling is per litre actually fed. At feed 50%, 10 L/s removes 1.2 K/s, giving a 12 K steady offset. All statuses update temperature. |
| Diesel | `HeavyIdealK=183`, `LightIdealK=157`, `BellHalfWidthK=22`, `YieldMax=0.85` | Truncated quadratic bell, not a Gaussian. Uses temperature, calibration and fouled flag at the start of the slice; the temperature update comes later. |
| Gas and residual tar | `GasBase=0.05`, `GasSlope=0.6`, `GasStartK=225`, `GasSpanK=60` | Gas is capped at `1-YDiesel`; tar is the remainder. Calibration/fouling losses therefore become tar under these formulas (discrepancy below). |
| Pressure | `BoilStartK=130`, `PressureGain=0.002`, `PressureFloorBar=1`, `VentBleedPerS=0.3`, `WarnBar=2.5` | Gain uses post-feed/post-flood charge and updated temperature. The specified unfed multiplier is 0.3. Valve bleed acts on pressure after gain, then pressure is floored. `WarnBar` is for the owner's warnings; it emits no event. |
| Room sources | `TrapLimit=0.6`, `RoomPpmPerVentExcess=40`, `RoomPpmPerGasL=0.05` | Excess vent fumes require charge and temperature above 130 K and scale by the specified clamped temperature factor over 100 K. Processed gas adds ppm independently. Input room ppm is fixed throughout the call; generated ppm is not fed back into later stills or slices. |
| Relief | `PopBar=3`, `PopPpm=60`, `PopSpillL=300` | At or above 3 bar after bleed, emit `Pop`, then its `Spill`. Spill is capped by remaining charge. The cask is lost once, pressure resets to 1, and previously made product remains. |
| Flash | `FlashK=320`, `FlashRoomPpmMin=40`, `FlashPpm=40`, `FlashConditionHit=30` | Temperature is strictly greater than 320 K, room ppm is inclusive at 40. Flash needs a Running still with positive remaining charge. Condition floors at zero; the fourth flash also emits `Failed`. Purge retains bath temperature. |
| Coke | `CokeHotK=290`, `CokeHotRate=0.6`, `CokeDryRate=0.3`, `CokeDryHeat=0.5`, `FouledCoke=60`, `FailedCoke=100`, `RepairCoke=40` | Hot and dry sources add together. Hot and heat thresholds are strict `>`. Neither source accrues while Failed. Coke clamps to 0..100; each upward fouling crossing emits once. Failure cuts heat steps to zero. Scrape/repair also update the required `bFouled` mirror. |
| Calibration | `CalDriftPerS=0.004`, `CalWarn=0.8` | Drift is per second when `Fed>0`, after processing and hazards. Loaded preheat with feed zero does not drift. `CalWarn` is an owner/UI threshold. Calibrate is accepted in all statuses except Failed, even when already calibrated. |
| Flood | `FloodFeed=0.8`, `FloodAfterSeconds=5`, `FloodSpillL=200`, `FloodCoolK=30` | Feed must be strictly above 80%. At 80% or below the counter resets. A spill removes at most remaining charge and cools the bath, retaining Running. Counter also resets whenever the still leaves Running. |
| Pay | `DieselCrPerL=0.5`, `CrudeSaleCr=40`, `TarFeeCrPer100L=10`, `MedicalFineCr=200` | Diesel credits round first; the tar fee applies only to complete hundreds of litres. Result pay is an integer and floors at zero. Negative critical count is treated as zero. Final conversion also rounds if a future owner supplies fractional credit coefficients. Ship bonuses/fines are not part of this floor calculation. |
| Fuel | `RefuelUnitsPerL=0.1`, `BaseFuelTonnesPer100L=1` | The ship fills first; missing units below zero or non-finite read as zero. Remaining litres become whole tonnes by flooring. Surplus has a zero floor solely to prevent floating-point roundoff from producing negative fuel after emptying the tank. |

Additional decisions where the text left details implicit:

- `Build` preserves the exact manifest order and resets all totals. Negative still count produces zero stills.
  `Rebuild` uses the original build manifest, not current waiting/hopper order. Manifest IDs are expected to
  be unique and non-None, as in the existing cargo manifest; there is no new ID validation policy.
- Rates used as divisors, sizes and time steps must remain positive; this is a simulation with valid rates,
  not a general validator for arbitrary rate sets. No default was tuned.
- Accepted knob operations return true even if clamping leaves the value unchanged. Zero step requests,
  unknown stills and invalid knob enums return false. Step addition widens to `int64` before clamping to
  avoid overflow on extreme integer input. Knob setters have no status gate, per the specified API.
- `State()` retains precise floats. The tick change detector compares copies floored to whole litres,
  temperature, coke, condition and flood seconds, tenths of a bar, and whole calibration percentages.
  Strings floor the requested values and format pressure to one decimal. Unknown readout returns empty.
- `OutRoomPpm` resets at each call; `OutEvents` is appended to, including preservation on rejected deltas.
  The tick return value concerns state publication, so the owner must consume ppm/events even when false.
- Completion with a queued cask retains Running and loads the next cask on the next slice. Without another
  queued cask it becomes Idle immediately. Batch yields remain available until the next cask is loaded;
  `BatchProgress` is zero when no cask is loaded. Since progress is `1-charge/1000`, spilled charge also
  advances that fraction; it is not a measurement of processed product.
- An unfinished loaded cask counts as lost in `FFloorResult` at the horn, in addition to losses already
  recorded in `State`. Waiting and hopper casks alone count as unrefined. This interprets the explicit
  "mid-batch is lost, not crude" rule while keeping `Tally` const.
- Event fields not used by the specified event remain zero. `BatchDone.Litres` is the sum of its three cuts;
  `Spill.Litres` is spilled crude. Cask identities are captured before clearing a charge, including the
  flash that also fails the unit. A failure with no loaded cask does not invent a cask loss.
- Floor-summary singulars follow the exact worked example (`1 spill`, `0 pops`, `1 critical`); other counts
  pluralize. There are no thousands separators. Gas and flashes remain result fields but are omitted from
  the specifically requested result-summary string.

## Discrepancies proved without changing the constants

These are passing tests of the specified arithmetic, not retuned approximations of the design prose.

| Claim in the brief/design | Measured behavior | Regression test (`Airlock.Refinery.Model.` prefix) |
| --- | --- | --- |
